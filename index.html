n8n · Gemini · Qdrant

AI Workflow Intelligence Suite

A collection of n8n workflows that turn a library of automation-workflow PDFs into a searchable knowledge base, then build business tools on top: market scoring, cost estimates, proposals, consulting reports, documentation, monitoring and more.

View source Back to portfolio

How it works

Describe a business need, get a ready-to-use deliverable.

1
You describe a need, e.g. "Build an AI invoice approval workflow".
2
The suite finds the closest workflow in the Qdrant knowledge base.
3
Gemini produces an estimate, proposal, consultation or optimisation plan.
4
The result is stored in Google Sheets / PDF.

Use it

Clone the repo, import the JSON files from workflows/ into n8n, add your own credentials (Gemini, Qdrant, Google Sheets) and run them in order.
